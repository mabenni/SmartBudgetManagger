# SmartBudget Manager

An Excel workbook that helps you record your **income and expenses**, sort expenses by **category**, and see your **monthly balance** and a **budget warning** at a glance.

## What it does
- Records income and expenses (Transactions sheet).
- Calculates Total Income, Total Expenses and Monthly Balance automatically.
- Compares spending with a monthly budget for each category.
- Shows **OVER BUDGET** in red when total spending exceeds the overall budget.
- Draws a pie chart of expenses by category.

## What you need
- Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible spreadsheet application).
- The file `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`. No internet connection and no macros.

## Get started
1. Double-click **SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx**. If a yellow bar says *Protected View*, click **Enable Editing**.
2. Open the **Categories** sheet and set your monthly budget for each category.
3. Open the **Transactions** sheet. In the first empty row, enter the **Date**, a **Description**, the **Type** (Income or Expense), the **Category** (expenses only) and the amount in the **Income** *or* **Expense** column.
4. Open the **Dashboard** sheet to see your totals, your balance, the budget status and the chart.
5. Save with **Ctrl+S**.

## The three worksheets
| Sheet | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Read totals, balance, budget status and the chart. Do not type here. |
| **Transactions** | Enter income and expenses (rows 4 to 50). |
| **Categories** | See the categories and change each monthly budget. |

## Good to know
- Do **not** type in the **Month** column or on the Dashboard: they are formulas.
- Choose the category **from the drop-down list**; otherwise the expense is not counted in the category totals.
- Totals read rows 4 to 50 (47 transactions) and **all** months entered. Use one file per month, or clear the old rows.
- Only the first five categories (Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education) appear in the Dashboard summary and the chart.
